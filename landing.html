Three agents, three ways to buy

Muse-style

Shops like Meta's Muse: the open web, Meta's product catalog and Facebook Marketplace.

Instinct-style

Shops like Instinct: independent Shopify stores that check out with Shop Pay.

Open web

A classic price comparer: the lowest total price from a major, reputable retailer.

Strategies are modelled on how Muse and Instinct source products. Boss runs its own agents; it does not connect to Muse or Instinct.

Nothing happens off the record

Every search, bid, verdict, approval and overrule is written to an audit trail you can open. When you overrule Boss, it remembers why and every agent hears about it next time. Boss never pays for you: approving opens the real product page after Boss re-checks the price and stock.
