How Boss runs your buying agents

One request goes in. Boss runs the agents on its own, and you make the final call.

  1. You ask, with your rules

    Say what you need, your budget and when. Add rules like "no sellers with poor reviews". If the request is too vague, Boss asks one question instead of guessing.

    Logged as request_received

  2. Boss briefs the agents

    Boss loads each agent's track record, your past lessons, and memories that match this request, then hands them to every agent.

    Memory from Cognee, track record from SingleStore

  3. Three agents search the live web

    Muse-style, Instinct-style and Open web each search with their own strategy and come back with one real product, price, seller and link. Every search they run is logged.

    Live web search by Claude

  4. Bids are reranked

    Each bid is scored against your request and rules before Boss sees it, so a cheap but off-target pick cannot win on price alone.

    Reranking by Superlinked SIE Serverless

  5. Boss judges and explains

    Boss rejects bids that break your rules and picks a winner, with a one-line reason and a score for every agent.

    Logged as bid_won, bid_rejected, decision_made

  6. You approve or overrule

    On approve, Boss re-checks the live price and stock, then opens the real product page. It never pays for you. On overrule, your reason becomes a lesson and that agent's trust drops.

    Lessons and approvals remembered in Cognee

  7. Everything stays on the record

    Every step is a row in the audit trail and an analytics event, and Boss is drilled against price jumps, wrong sizes, sold-out sellers and vague requests.

    Trail in SingleStore, events in Amplitude, drills with Firedrill
